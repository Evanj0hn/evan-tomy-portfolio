About Me

A little about my journey

I like working across the stack — low-level enough to configure a microcontroller or read a Wireshark capture, and high-level enough to ship a Flask API or a Godot game. That range comes from a mix of classwork and hands-on roles that kept pointing me at different layers of the same problem: how do you take messy, real-world input and turn it into something reliable?

Outside the classroom, I co-founded ConHacks, Conestoga College's first student-run hackathon, and I've worked as a contract data engineer cleaning diagnostic lab data and as a systems intern supporting IT operations for a global team. I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Science (Honours) at Conestoga College.
